# NicheAI: Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines how NicheAI acquires, activates, and retains its core users. In the fast-paced 2026 AI landscape, distribution is a first-class product feature, not an afterthought.

---

## 1. Target User Personas

We focus deeply on two specific personas to prevent building a generic, horizontal tool.

### Persona 1: The Bootstrapped Indie Hacker

- **Profile:** A highly technical solo founder building SaaS in their evenings/weekends. Strong coding skills, weak product marketing and validation skills.
- **Job To Be Done (JTBD):** "Help me figure out if this idea is a tarpit before I waste 3 months coding it."
- **Pain Point:** Analysis paralysis and building features no one wants.
- **Aha! Moment:** Entering a vague idea and watching NicheAI instantly generate a visual competitor matrix proving the market is saturated, then pivoting them to a defensible micro-niche.

### Persona 2: The Seed-Stage Product Manager

- **Profile:** The first product hire at a newly funded startup. Overwhelmed by feature requests from the CEO and struggling to prioritize the roadmap.
- **Job To Be Done (JTBD):** "Give me data-backed ammunition to say 'no' to bad feature ideas and align the team on a strategic roadmap."
- **Pain Point:** Spending days manually scraping G2 reviews and competitor websites to build slide decks.
- **Aha! Moment:** Exporting an auto-generated, highly detailed Product Requirement Document (PRD) directly into Linear, backed by real-time competitor gap analysis.

## 5. Distribution Mechanics (Built-In Growth)

We do not rely solely on paid acquisition. Distribution is engineered into the product.

### Programmatic SEO (P-SEO) & Content Generation

- **The Engine:** NicheAI automatically scrapes public SaaS product data and generates highly optimized "Head-to-Head Comparison" landing pages (e.g., _Stripe vs. LemonSqueezy: The 2026 Breakdown_).
- **The Hook:** These pages rank on Google for high-intent comparison queries. The call-to-action (CTA) allows visitors to "Generate an AI Strategy to beat both of them."

### "Export to Flex" (Viral Loops)

- **The Engine:** Founders naturally want to build in public and share their progress on X (Twitter) and LinkedIn.
- **The Hook:** NicheAI includes a 1-click "Generate Shareable Insights Card" feature. This creates a beautifully designed, branded image summarizing their market thesis, which includes a subtle "Validated by NicheAI" watermark.

### Hybrid Product-Led Growth (PLG) + Sales

1.  **Bottom-Up (PLG):** The Indie Hacker persona enters via the $29/mo self-serve tier. The UX is entirely frictionless.
2.  **Product-Qualified Leads (PQLs):** The system monitors workspace activity. If a workspace invites 3+ members or connects a corporate Jira/Linear instance, the account is flagged.
3.  **Top-Down (Sales):** The founding team reaches out directly to the PQL offering an upgrade to the Enterprise tier, emphasizing dedicated ML pipelines and priority syncs.
