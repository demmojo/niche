# NicheAI: Business Plan & Market Strategy

This document serves as the foundational business model, pricing strategy, and competitive landscape definition for NicheAI. It is the primary reference for Go-To-Market (GTM) motions and financial modeling.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

NicheAI is an autonomous market intelligence platform and AI product strategist built for founders, indie hackers, and small product teams. It solves the pervasive problem of building "tarpit" ideas (broad, horizontal tools competing in saturated markets) by guiding users to discover, validate, and execute on hyper-niche, defensible product opportunities using real-time competitor data.

## 2. Market Sizing (The "TAM/SAM/SOM")

Our market sizing focuses exclusively on digital product builders, software founders, and early-stage startup teams.

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** ~$15B. The global market for product management software, competitive intelligence tools, and early-stage startup enablement platforms.
- **Serviceable Available Market (SAM):** ~$3.5B. Bootstrapped founders, YC-style early-stage software startups, and independent digital creators actively seeking product-market fit.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** ~$150M. First-time and repeat technical solo-founders/indie hackers who build in public, utilizing AI to augment missing product management or strategic marketing skills.

## 3. Business Model & Pricing Strategy

NicheAI employs a hybrid monetization strategy designed to lower the barrier to entry for solo founders while capturing the immense value generated by our autonomous AI pipelines.

### Pricing Tiers (SaaS + Usage-Based)

| Tier                    | Target Persona           | Monthly Price  | Core Features                                                                               | Limits                                                                       |
| :---------------------- | :----------------------- | :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bootstrapper**        | Indie Hackers, Solo Devs | $29/mo         | Competitor tracking (3 products), Strategy Dashboards, Basic Lens Insights.                 | 50 AI generation credits/mo, basic data refresh (weekly).                    |
| **Scale-Up**            | Funded Seed Teams, PMs   | $99/mo         | Unlimited competitor tracking, Visual Intelligence Whiteboards, OpenSpec Export.            | 250 AI generation credits/mo, priority data refresh (daily), Multi-Seat (3). |
| **Enterprise Strategy** | Innovation Labs, Studios | Custom ($499+) | Custom taxonomy merging, private data ingestion (PDF/Internal DBs), dedicated ML pipelines. | Unlimited credits, real-time trigger syncs, dedicated account manager.       |

### Unit Economics & Cost Drivers

- **Primary Cost Center:** External AI API calls (Gemini 3.1 Pro for deep reasoning, Gemini Flash for high-throughput extraction) and data enrichment APIs (Apollo.io, Exa).
- **Cost Mitigation Strategy:**
  - **Tiering:** Heavy compute operations (like full market deep-dives) consume credits. Standard UI operations rely entirely on cached database reads (Firestore/Supabase).
  - **Heuristics First:** We aggressively route simple classification tasks to deterministic code or edge-deployed Small Language Models (SLMs) to bypass LLM token costs.

## 5. The Data Flywheel (Our Long-Term Moat)

NicheAI becomes smarter and more proprietary the more it is used. We do not just pass data through an LLM; we capture interaction.

1.  **AI Proposes Strategy:** The Lens Product Strategist drafts a feature or identifies a market gap.
2.  **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Adjudication:** The founder accepts, edits, or explicitly rejects the suggestion.
3.  **Telemetry Capture:** Rejections and edits are logged as high-signal intent data.
4.  **System Evolution:** Future prompt formulations for that specific user (and aggregated across similar cohorts) are automatically adjusted, lowering the hallucination rate and increasing the strategic hit rate over time.
