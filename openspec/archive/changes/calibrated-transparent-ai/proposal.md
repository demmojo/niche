# Proposal: Calibrated Transparent AI

## Why

As NicheAI establishes itself as a proprietary, closed-source SaaS, building trust with founders inputting highly-guarded intellectual property (startup ideas) is paramount. "Black box" AI models fail to provide the necessary assurances. Users require verifiable proof of data safety, precise citations for AI-generated claims to combat hallucination fears, and clear visibility into the "why" behind strategic recommendations.

## What Changes

We are implementing "Calibrated Trust"—a suite of five UX and Backend patterns:

1. Cryptographically Verifiable Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Receipts.
2. Granular RAG Provenance & Source Anchoring.
3. Explainable Rationale via Top-Driver Attribution.
4. Counterfactual Sliders for boundary testing.
5. The Autonomy Dial for granular consent over Agentic AI actions.

## Capabilities

- **Mathematical Proof of Ephemerality:** Generate SHA-256 receipts confirming sensitive prompts are instantly destroyed.
- **Interactive Source Citations:** AI claims are tied to specific Exa/Apollo data, with unedited source text visible on hover.
- **Strategic Transparency:** Score breakdown drawers and counterfactual "what-if" sliders allow users to interact with and understand the AI's logic.
- **Controlled Autonomy:** Users dictate AI independence levels (Co-Pilot vs. Auto-Pilot) and approve actions via a centralized Audit Log.

## Impact

This feature set will drastically reduce user hesitation regarding IP upload, mitigate churn caused by hallucination fears, and position NicheAI as a securely verifiable intelligence platform despite being closed-source.
