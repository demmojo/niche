"Developers are tired of configuring AWS"

Recent discussions on HackerNews and Twitter reveal a growing frustration with infrastructure complexity. Indie hackers are spending days configuring terraform instead of shipping features.

While PaaS solutions like Vercel exist, they often abstract too much. Users want a middle ground: One-click deployment that doesn't hide the underlying Dockerfiles. They want the convenience of serverless with the control of a VPS.

The pricing model of existing tools is also a major pain point. Predictable, flat-rate pricing is highly desired over complex usage-based models.
